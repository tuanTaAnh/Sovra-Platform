# Troubleshooting FAQ Sample

## Low Tire Pressure Warning
If the tire pressure warning appears, reduce speed and avoid sharp steering or hard braking. Park safely and check all tires using a reliable tire pressure gauge. Inflate tires to the recommended pressure shown on the tire pressure label or in the vehicle manual.

If the warning remains after inflation, inspect for punctures or damage and contact roadside assistance or a service center. Do not continue driving on a visibly flat tire.

## Charging Port Does Not Open
If the charging port does not open, make sure the vehicle is unlocked. Try opening the charging port from the infotainment screen or key fob. In cold weather, ice may prevent the port from opening; gently clear the area and try again.

## Battery Warning Light
A battery warning light can indicate a problem with the charging system or electrical system. Safely stop when possible and consult the vehicle manual. If the warning remains, contact service support.
