# Troubleshooting FAQ Sample

## Low Tire Pressure Warning

If the tire pressure warning appears, reduce speed and avoid sharp steering or hard braking. Park safely and check all tires using a reliable tire pressure gauge.

Inflate tires to the recommended pressure shown on the tire pressure label or in the vehicle manual. The recommended pressure is the vehicle manufacturer's recommended pressure, not necessarily the number printed on the tire sidewall.

For the most accurate reading, check tire pressure when tires are cold. If the vehicle has recently been driven, recheck the pressure later when the tires are cold.

If the warning remains after inflation, inspect for punctures, visible damage, uneven wear, or a visibly flat tire. Contact roadside assistance or a service center if damage is found.

Do not continue driving on a visibly flat tire. Driving on a flat tire can damage the tire and wheel and may reduce vehicle control.

## Tire Pressure Warning Flashes Then Stays On

If the tire pressure warning flashes for about one minute and then stays on, the tire pressure monitoring system may have a malfunction. Check tire pressure manually if safe, then contact a service center for inspection.

A tire pressure monitoring system is a warning aid. It does not replace regular tire pressure checks.

## Charging Port Does Not Open

If the charging port does not open, make sure the vehicle is unlocked. Try opening the charging port from the infotainment screen, key fob, or charging menu.

In cold weather, ice may prevent the port from opening. Gently clear the area around the charging port and try again. Do not force the charging door open.

If the charging port remains stuck, stop attempting to force it and contact service support.

## Battery Warning Light

A battery warning light can indicate a problem with the charging system or electrical system. Safely stop when possible and consult the vehicle manual.

If the warning remains, contact service support. Avoid continuing a long trip with an active battery warning unless the vehicle manual says it is safe.

## Brake Warning

If a brake warning appears, reduce speed carefully and stop in a safe location. Check whether the parking brake is engaged. If the warning remains after the parking brake is released, contact service support before continuing.

## Overheating Warning

If an overheating warning appears, reduce speed, avoid hard acceleration, and stop safely when possible. Let the vehicle cool and follow the vehicle manual. Do not open hot coolant components.

## Troubleshooting Assistant Guidance

When answering troubleshooting questions, prioritize safety first, then explain next steps. If the local documents do not contain the requested warning, say that the local documents do not provide enough information.
