# EV Charging Guide Sample

## Battery Efficiency

To improve EV battery efficiency, drive smoothly, avoid unnecessary hard acceleration, use regenerative braking when possible, and precondition the cabin while the vehicle is still plugged in.

Keeping tire pressure within the recommended range can also improve range. Extra cargo, roof boxes, aggressive acceleration, very high speed, steep roads, and extreme temperatures can reduce driving range.

Use Eco mode when appropriate. Eco mode may reduce energy consumption by smoothing acceleration and optimizing climate control.

## Regenerative Braking

Regenerative braking helps recover energy when the vehicle slows down. Use regenerative braking smoothly and anticipate traffic to reduce unnecessary braking.

Regenerative braking may feel different depending on battery temperature, battery charge level, road conditions, and selected drive mode.

## Daily Charging

For daily use, many EVs are most efficient when charged to a moderate level rather than 100 percent every day. Follow the vehicle manufacturer's recommendation for the preferred daily charge limit.

If the vehicle allows a daily charge limit, set the limit in the charging menu. For long trips, charging to a higher level may be useful before departure.

## Fast Charging

Fast charging is useful for long trips, but frequent fast charging may increase battery temperature and long-term wear. If the vehicle supports battery preconditioning, enable navigation to a fast charger so the battery can reach an optimal charging temperature before arrival.

Fast charging speed may slow down when the battery is very cold, very hot, or nearly full. This is normal behavior for many EVs.

## Charging Schedule

A charging schedule can help start charging during preferred electricity hours. Open the charging menu, choose Charging Schedule, set the departure time or start time, and confirm that the charging cable is connected.

If scheduled charging does not start, check the cable connection, charger status, charge limit, and whether the schedule is enabled.

## EV Charging Guidance

When answering EV charging questions, distinguish daily charging, trip charging, fast charging, and efficiency tips. If the local documents do not contain a specific charging limit or battery percentage, say that the local documents do not provide enough information.
