# Vehicle Manual Assistant Sample

## Lane Assist

Lane Assist helps keep the vehicle centered within clearly marked lane boundaries. To enable Lane Assist, open Vehicle Settings, select Driver Assistance, then enable Lane Assist.

The system works best when lane markings are visible, the road surface is clear, and the windshield camera area is clean. Lane Assist may be limited in heavy rain, snow, fog, construction zones, faded lane markings, sharp curves, or when the camera is blocked.

If Lane Assist is unavailable, check that the windshield camera area is not blocked by dirt, snow, stickers, or condensation. Clean the camera area if needed and restart the vehicle system. If the issue remains, contact a service center.

Lane Assist is a driver support feature only. The driver must always keep hands on the wheel, watch the road, follow local traffic laws, and remain responsible for vehicle control.

## Bluetooth Phone Connection

To connect a phone, open the vehicle infotainment system, select Phone, then Add New Device. Enable Bluetooth on the phone and select the vehicle name from the phone's Bluetooth menu.

Confirm that the pairing code shown on the vehicle display matches the code shown on the phone. After pairing, allow contacts and media permissions if you want calling, contacts, and audio playback.

If pairing fails, remove the old vehicle profile from the phone, restart Bluetooth, and try pairing again. If the phone was previously connected to another vehicle, forget the old connection before retrying.

If audio does not play after pairing, check that the correct audio source is selected and that media audio permission is enabled on the phone.

## Infotainment Shortcuts

The infotainment system provides quick access to Phone, Navigation, Charging, Climate, Driver Assistance, and Vehicle Settings. Some functions may be disabled while driving to reduce distraction.

For safety, avoid changing complex settings while the vehicle is moving. Use voice commands where available or ask a passenger to help.

## Driver Assistance Safety

Driver assistance features are support systems only. They do not replace driver attention. Always keep control of the vehicle.

Adaptive cruise, lane support, parking assistance, and warning systems can help the driver, but they may not detect every vehicle, pedestrian, cyclist, lane marking, object, or road condition.

If a warning appears, respond safely and do not rely only on the system. If a driver assistance feature behaves unexpectedly, turn it off when safe and contact service support.

## Vehicle Manual Q&A Guidance

When answering vehicle manual questions, give clear step-by-step instructions. If the local manual does not contain the requested feature, say that the local documents do not provide enough information.
