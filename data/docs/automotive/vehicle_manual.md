# Vehicle Manual Assistant Sample

## Lane Assist
Lane Assist helps keep the vehicle centered within clearly marked lane boundaries. To enable Lane Assist, open Vehicle Settings, select Driver Assistance, then enable Lane Assist. The system works best when lane markings are visible and the camera area is clean.

If Lane Assist is unavailable, check that the windshield camera area is not blocked by dirt, snow, stickers, or condensation. The driver must always keep hands on the wheel and remain responsible for vehicle control.

## Bluetooth Phone Connection
To connect a phone, open the vehicle infotainment system, select Phone, then Add New Device. Enable Bluetooth on the phone and select the vehicle name from the phone's Bluetooth menu. Confirm that the pairing code shown on the vehicle display matches the code shown on the phone.

If pairing fails, remove the old vehicle profile from the phone, restart Bluetooth, and try pairing again.

## Driver Assistance Safety
Driver assistance features are support systems only. They do not replace driver attention. Always follow local traffic laws and keep control of the vehicle.
